Lanternlight

A hand-shadow theatre for your room

Sit comfortably and face a wall. A little paper lantern lights up beside you and turns your wall into a stage. No free wall nearby? A paper scroll unrolls in front of you instead.

Your hands are the puppets. Raise one into the light. Make shapes, feed the crocodile, build a bridge for the rabbit. Controllers work too: they become shadow puppets on sticks.

Tap the lantern any time to pause or change settings.

Built with Meta's Immersive Web SDK · built with AI assistance (Claude Code)
Everything stays on your headset. No account, no camera images, no tracking.
