Lanternlight

Every wall is a stage, once the lantern is lit.

  1. Sit facing a wall.
  2. Raise your hands into the light.
  3. Make shapes. The shadows will do the rest.
Settings

Rest a fingertip on the lantern at any time to pause or change these. Controllers work too: they become shadow puppets on sticks.
