# Lanternlight: tester guide (about 20 minutes)

Thank you for trying Lanternlight. It's a hand-shadow theatre: a paper lantern lights up beside
you, your wall becomes a stage, and your hands cast shadows that come to life.

## Before you start (2 minutes)

1. **Which headset do you have?** Please tell us, because the models behave differently:
   - **Quest 3 or 3S**: white, the newest. Your room looks **in colour** through the headset.
   - **Quest 2**: white. Your room looks **black-and-white** through the headset. That's fine; the show works the same.
   - **Original Quest (2019)**: **black**. It can't show your room, so Lanternlight runs in a
     dim "virtual theatre" instead. That's expected; please still try everything.
2. **Run Space Setup first** (it lets the app find your walls): in *Settings*, look for
   *Physical space → Space Setup* (on some versions *Environment setup* or *Room setup*) and
   follow the steps: confirm your walls and mark your desk or table. Menu names differ between
   headset software versions, so ask us if you can't find it. If you skip it, the show still works on a
   paper scroll; please tell us whether you ran it.
3. **Sit down facing a wall** 1 to 3 metres away, if you can. A desk in front of you is fine.
4. **Turn on hand tracking**: in *Settings*, find *Movement tracking* (or *Hands and controllers*) and switch hand tracking on.

## Open the test link

1. Put the controllers down. Open the **Browser** in the headset.
2. Go to **https://lanternfields.github.io/lanternlight/?tester=1**
   (type it exactly, including `?tester=1` at the end).
3. Press **Begin**. If the headset asks for permission to use your room / hand tracking /
   spatial data, choose **Allow**.
   - On an **original Quest** the button says **Begin (virtual theatre)**. That's expected.
   - If it says **Open on Meta Quest**, the page isn't open in the headset's own Browser.
4. A small **Tester** card floats beside the lantern. It shows FPS, which headset and mode you
   are on, and a checklist. If you can, keep it in a screenshot or recording now and then.

## What to try (ticks itself on the Tester card)

- **Make the rabbit.** Raise a hand into the light; when a faint ghost hand shows a "peace" sign, copy it.
- **Feed the crocodile.** Hold your shadow on a glowing circle to open a tale. Open your hand,
  then pinch your thumb and fingers together to bite the fireflies.
- **Rabbit's bridge.** Hold your hand flat and sideways so its shadow makes a bridge over the
  river. For the last river, bring your hand closer to the lantern to make the shadow bigger.
- **Use both hands** at some point (either hand can perform).
- **Try controllers**: pick them up. They become shadow puppets on sticks, and the trigger bites.
- **Lantern menu**: rest a fingertip on the lantern for about half a second. Try *Dim*, and
  *Stage here* (look at another spot first, then press it).
- **The Moon Wolf**: when the wolf creeps toward the rabbit, make your shadow big by bringing
  your hand close to the lantern, or raise both hands.

## Recording (please, if you can)

- Open the system menu (the **Meta button** on the right controller, or look at your palm and
  pinch), find **Camera** (or the *Record* tile in quick settings), and start **Record video**.
  Record 1 to 3 minutes of play, then stop recording the same way. Exact menu names vary by
  headset software version.
- Videos land in the headset's **Files / Gallery**. Share via the **Meta Horizon phone app**
  (*Devices → Gallery / Media*, or it auto-syncs to the app), then send us the file or a link.
- A couple of screenshots of the Tester card are very helpful too.

## Six quick questions

1. **Hand shadows:** did your shadow follow your hand closely and feel like *your* shadow? (1–5)
2. **Smoothness:** any stutter, lag or flicker? When? What FPS did the Tester card show?
3. **Placement:** did the light appear on your wall (or on a paper scroll)? Was it a good spot? Did *Stage here* fix it?
4. **Confusing moments:** anything you didn't know how to do, or that happened by accident?
5. **Comfort:** any strain (arms, eyes, neck), or brightness/dimming issues?
6. **Fun:** how much did you enjoy it? (1–5) Would you show it to someone?

Also tell us: headset model, whether you ran Space Setup, hands or controllers, and roughly how
far your wall was. Thank you!
