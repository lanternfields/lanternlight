# Lanternlight: tester guide (about 20 minutes)

Thank you for trying Lanternlight. It's a hand-shadow theatre: a paper lantern lights up beside
you, your wall becomes a stage, and your hands cast shadows that come to life.

> **Updated 28 Oct.** What changed since the first version of this guide:
> 1. The start button is now called **Light the lantern** (it used to say *Begin*).
> 2. To make a shadow **bigger**, bring your hand **back toward you** (not toward the lantern).
> 3. **Stage here** (lantern menu): press it, then look where you want the stage and hold still for a moment.
> 4. On **Quest 2** your room looks black and white. That's expected: the lantern light and the shadows still show in colour.
> 5. A storyteller now speaks in short lines on a paper ribbon, and short hints (like *Raise a hand into the light*) appear only if you pause. You can turn on **Full captions** under *Settings* for longer explanations.
> 6. **The Moon Wolf** is now one big scare: make your shadow big once, and watch what the wolf does next.
> 7. **Rabbit's Bridge**: if nobody helps for a while, a paper plank drops in so the rabbit always gets home.
>
> **Updated 29 Oct.** What changed since 28 Oct:
> 1. **New story text** everywhere on the stage: the storyteller's lines and the short hints are all new (for example *Reach into the light*, *Make two long ears*).
> 2. **A new ending, "The Last Light".** After all three tales, a little paper bird flits about looking for somewhere to land. **Hold up one finger and keep it very still**: the bird lands on your finger's shadow. Then watch where the light goes. If you don't, the bird lands on the ground after a while.
> 3. The tales are now called **The Hungry River**, **The Broken Bridge** and **The Moon Wolf**. Pip the rabbit watches the crocodile from the reeds, and the wolf is taller and thinner.
> 4. In **The Moon Wolf** there is no ring around the moon any more: as your shadow grows, the wolf's ears flatten, Pip trembles and the light gets brighter.
> 5. The crocodile's fireflies are bigger, and each one you catch keeps glowing in its throat.

## Before you start (2 minutes)

1. **Which headset do you have?** Please tell us, because the models behave differently:
   - **Quest 3 or 3S**: white, the newest. Your room looks **in colour** through the headset.
   - **Quest 2**: white. Your room looks **black-and-white** through the headset. That's expected:
     the lantern's warm light and the shadows are drawn by the app, so they still show in colour
     on your grey wall, and the show works the same.
   - **Original Quest (2019)**: **black**. It can't show your room, so Lanternlight runs in a
     dim "virtual theatre" instead. That's expected; please still try everything.
2. **Run Space Setup first** (it lets the app find your walls): in *Settings*, look for
   *Physical space → Space Setup* (on some versions *Environment setup* or *Room setup*) and
   follow the steps: confirm your walls and mark your desk or table. Menu names differ between
   headset software versions, so ask us if you can't find it. If you skip it, a few seconds in the
   app says *Let's find your walls* and opens the headset's own room setup; finish it and the
   show comes back on your wall. If you cancel, the show still works on a paper scroll. Please
   tell us whether you ran it.
3. **Sit down facing a wall** about 0.6 to 4.5 metres away. A desk (even with a monitor) in
   front of you is fine, and if there's a table or desk within reach the lantern sits on it.
4. **Turn on hand tracking**: in *Settings*, find *Movement tracking* (or *Hands and controllers*) and switch hand tracking on.

## Open the test link

1. Put the controllers down. Open the **Browser** in the headset.
2. Go to **https://lanternfields.github.io/lanternlight/?tester=1**
   (type it exactly, including `?tester=1` at the end).
3. Press **Light the lantern**. If the headset asks for permission to use your room / hand tracking /
   spatial data, choose **Allow**.
   - On an **original Quest** a note under the button says the show plays in a little virtual
     theatre. That's expected.
   - If it says **Open on Meta Quest**, the page isn't open in the headset's own Browser.
4. A small **Tester** card floats beside the lantern. It shows FPS, which headset and mode you
   are on, and a checklist. If you can, keep it in a screenshot or recording now and then.

## What to try (ticks itself on the Tester card)

- **Make the rabbit.** Raise a hand into the light; when a faint ghost hand shows a "peace" sign, copy it.
- **Feed the crocodile** (*The Hungry River*). Hold your shadow on a glowing circle to open a
  tale. Open your hand, then pinch your thumb and fingers together to bite the fireflies.
- **Rabbit's bridge** (*The Broken Bridge*). Hold your hand flat and sideways so its shadow
  makes a bridge over the river. (If nobody helps for a while, a paper plank drops in, so the
  rabbit always gets home.)
- **Use both hands** at some point (either hand can perform).
- **Try controllers**: pick them up. They become shadow puppets on sticks, and the trigger bites.
- **Lantern menu**: rest a fingertip on the lantern for about half a second. Try *Dim*, and
  *Stage here* (press it, then look at where you want the stage and hold still for a moment).
- **The Moon Wolf**: when the wolf creeps toward the rabbit, make your shadow big by bringing
  your hand back toward you (toward your chest), or raise both hands.
- **The Last Light** (after all three tales): hold up one finger and keep it very still until
  the bird lands on it. Then just watch, all the way to *Goodnight*. To start again, reach into
  the light.

## Recording (please, if you can)

- Open the system menu (the **Meta button** on the right controller, or look at your palm and
  pinch), find **Camera** (or the *Record* tile in quick settings), and start **Record video**.
  Record 1 to 3 minutes of play, then stop recording the same way. Exact menu names vary by
  headset software version.
- Videos land in the headset's **Files / Gallery**. Share via the **Meta Horizon phone app**
  (*Devices → Gallery / Media*, or it auto-syncs to the app), then send us the file or a link.
- A couple of screenshots of the Tester card are very helpful too.

## Six quick questions

1. **Hand shadows:** did your shadow follow your hand closely and feel like *your* shadow? (1–5)
2. **Smoothness:** any stutter, lag or flicker? When? What FPS did the Tester card show?
3. **Placement:** did the light appear on your wall (or on a paper scroll)? Was it a good spot? Did *Stage here* fix it?
4. **Confusing moments:** anything you didn't know how to do, or that happened by accident?
5. **Comfort:** any strain (arms, eyes, neck), or brightness/dimming issues?
6. **Fun:** how much did you enjoy it? (1–5) Would you show it to someone?

Also tell us: headset model, whether you ran Space Setup, hands or controllers, and roughly how
far your wall was. Thank you!
